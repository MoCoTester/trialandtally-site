Count trials, not paperwork.

Trial&Tally is a simple iPhone and iPad app for tallying trial-by-trial accuracy on student goals during a live session. Tap correct, incorrect, or prompted; see each goal's accuracy; copy the summary onto your paper data sheet; then clear everything.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Up to 6 students and 5 goals each, identified only by 2 initials.
  • One tap per trial: Correct, Incorrect, or Prompted (with the prompt type, such as visual, verbal, or physical). Only “correct on own” counts as correct.
  • Accuracy for every goal as a whole-number percentage, with multi-step undo for each student and an option to hide percentages from view.
  • A clean summary to copy onto paper, or share as text, PDF, or image, or print. A privacy reminder appears before anything is shared.
  • End & Clear erases the whole session when you're done.
  • Large buttons, color plus text labels (never color alone), Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver support. The screen stays awake during a session.

Who it's for

Speech-language pathologists, occupational therapists, special education teachers, ABA therapists, tutors, and anyone who takes trial-by-trial data during a session.

The privacy promise

  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No cloud. Session data stays on your device and is excluded from iCloud backup.
  • Nothing kept after End & Clear. There is no session history to leak.
  • No analytics, ads, or tracking of any kind.
