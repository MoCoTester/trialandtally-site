How it works

    
From the first student through the door to a clean slate.

    

      
    
        
  1. 1
    Start a session in one tap
    Tap New Session and you’re in. Add students as they arrive: 1 or 2 initials and up to 5 goals each, up to 6 students per session.
    2. 
        
  2. 2
    One tap per trial
    Big Correct, Prompted and Incorrect buttons for every goal. The percentage updates with each tap, and Undo takes back a slip.
    3. 
        
  3. 3
    Track prompt levels as you go
    Tap Prompted, pick the main prompt (Visual, Verbal, Physical or your own), then Correct or Incorrect. Only correct on their own counts toward accuracy.
    4. 
        
  4. 4
    Your data sheet, minus the math
    Accuracy, counts and a prompt breakdown for every goal, ready to copy onto your paper data sheet. Or share it as text or PDF, or print it.
    5. 
        
  5. 5
    End & Clear. Nothing kept.
    When you’re done, End & Clear erases the whole session from the device after an “Are you sure?”. No history, no cloud copy.

Questions

      
Is it a subscription?
No. After the 7-day free trial, a one-time $4.99 purchase unlocks Trial&Tally for good. With Family Sharing, your family gets the unlock too.

      
What happens when the trial ends?
You can’t start new sessions until you unlock. A session that’s already open can keep recording until midnight that day, and after that you can still view, share and clear it. You never lose data you’ve already recorded.

      
Does it need the internet?
No. Sessions work fully offline. You only need a connection to start the free trial, buy or restore the unlock, and open the support and rating links.

      
Can I change the prompt types and button labels?
Yes. In Settings you can rename, add, reorder and delete prompt types, and rename the Correct, Prompted and Incorrect buttons.

      
How many students and goals?
Up to 6 students per session and up to 5 goals per student. Add students and goals any time during the session.
