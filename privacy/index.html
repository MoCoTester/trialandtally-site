Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026

Trial&Tally collects no data. In App Store terms: Data Not Collected.

Who we are

Trial&Tally is operated by Gabriel Hamby, Cumming, Georgia, USA (“we”). Questions: support@trialandtally.com.

Session data stays on your device

  • Everything you enter during a session (student initials, goals, trials, and notes) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The session file is excluded from iCloud and device backups and protected with iOS file protection (after a restart, it stays encrypted and unreadable until you first unlock the device).
  • End & Clear deletes it, including any temporary files made for sharing. Deleting the app also deletes it. Nothing is kept after that, and there is no session history.

What we don't do

No accounts or sign-in. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No third-party SDKs or code. The app does not connect to any server of ours; we don't run one.

Settings

Your app preferences (such as student and goal limits, custom button labels, prompt types, and appearance) are stored on your device like any app's settings and contain no student data.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. To apply the trial rules, the app reads Apple's signed record of your purchases on the device and remembers your trial start date there.

Crash reports

We receive crash reports and diagnostics only if you opt in to sharing them with app developers in your device's settings (Apple's “Share With App Developers”). These come to us through Apple, without student data, and are used only to fix problems.

Sharing summaries

A session summary leaves your device only when you choose to share it (as text, PDF, or image, or by printing). The app shows a privacy reminder first. Where it goes is up to you and is handled by the app or service you pick.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to respond. We don't add you to lists or share your email. Emails sent from the app's Send Feedback button include the app version and device model, which you can delete before sending. Please never include student names or information in emails. Support email is delivered through our email provider (Google).

Students and children

Trial&Tally is intended for adult professionals, not for children to use. Students are identified only by initials that you type, and that information never leaves your device unless you share a summary. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and because student information never leaves your device unless you share it, we never receive or have access to student records.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
