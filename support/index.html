Frequently asked questions

Why is there no sync, cloud backup, or session history?

By design, for student privacy. Trial&Tally is meant for one live session at a time: you tally, copy the summary onto your paper data sheet (or share it), then tap End & Clear. Keeping student data off servers and out of long-term storage means there is nothing to sync, lose, or leak.

Does Trial&Tally work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. An internet connection is only needed to start the free trial, buy or restore the unlock, and open the support and rating links.

How does the free 7-day trial work?

Tap Start Free Trial in the app. This is a free ($0) in-app purchase through Apple, so no payment is taken. The 7 days count from the moment you start it, and the trial includes everything. The app shows how many days are left.

What happens when the trial ends?

You can no longer start new sessions. If a session is open when the trial ends, you can keep recording until midnight that day; after that it can only be viewed, shared, and cleared, so you never lose data you've already recorded. A one-time $4.99 unlock removes all limits forever. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new or reset device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Your unlock (and your trial start date) come back. Restoring cannot restart the free trial.

Does the unlock work with Family Sharing?

Yes. If you use Apple's Family Sharing, the $4.99 unlock is shared with your family members. (The free trial itself is not shared; each person starts their own.)

What does End & Clear do?

It permanently deletes the whole session from the device: students, goals, trials, notes, and any temporary files made for sharing. The app asks “Are you sure?” first. It cannot be undone, so copy or share your summary before you clear.

Will I lose my data if I close the app or my device locks?

No. The session is saved on the device after every tap and survives closing the app, locking the screen, and restarting the device. It stays until you tap End & Clear. Nothing is cleared automatically. (Deleting the app also deletes its data.)

Should I keep automatic updates on?

Yes, we recommend keeping automatic App Store updates on (Settings → App Store → App Updates) so you get fixes and improvements. Trial&Tally never connects to the internet to check for updates itself. Updates keep your open session.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, in English and Spanish. The app follows your device's language.
